Game Overview

Super Body quest, build your knowledge on dieting, minerals, macro and micro nutrients. Learn about exercising an dimportance of keeping a healthy lifestyle. Age range is recommended to be around 8-13 years old.

In this game, you’ll complete the puzzle by answering questions and completing challenges to unlock every puzzle piece.

How to Play

  1. : Choose one of the categories (Food fact, Action challenge, and Mindful Moment.)
  2. : Answer the question/or do the task it suggest
  3. : Answer three questions correct to unlock one puzzle piece
  4. : Unlock all puzzle pieces to finish the puzzle
  5. : Reise on the questions you got wrong and play again!
